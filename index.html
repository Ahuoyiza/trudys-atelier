The atelier

Cut for our climate, our bodies and our light

Trudy’s Atelier began with a simple frustration: minimalist fashion rarely had us in mind. So we design for the African woman first, with breathable fabrics for real heat, proportions drafted on real bodies, and tones chosen to glow against deep skin.

Everything is made in small runs in our Lagos studio, finished by hand, and designed to be worn for years rather than seasons.

“I wanted clothes that feel like exhaling. Nothing tight, nothing loud, nothing you have to think about.” Trudy, founder & creative director
